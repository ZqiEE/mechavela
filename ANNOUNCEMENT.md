# Introducing Mechavela: a robotics workspace for your own AI

Describe what you want to build. Let your AI write the code, call real engineering tools, inspect actual results and revise the design.

Mechavela brings project files, editable CAD, detailed assembly visualization, engineering checks and simulation interfaces into one local robotics workspace. It builds on FreeCAD/Open CASCADE, MuJoCo and Three.js rather than inventing another CAD or physics engine.

The Windows source technology preview is now available. Bring your own AI and configure the external engineering tools. Start with a blank project; help us improve the setup and everyday workflow.

Repository: https://github.com/ZqiEE/mechavela

This is an early preview, not an automatically manufacturable robot generator or a signed all-in-one installer. See the verified scope and setup requirements in the repository.
